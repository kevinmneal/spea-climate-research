# Exploratory biogeography and the next biological questions

**29 October status:** the [integrated comparative manuscript](../manuscript/README.md) is now the active paper and the exploratory analysis phase is complete. The questions below remain background and conditional future research directions; this public update initiates no new analysis or acquisition.

This module describes the wider research program as of 21 October 2026. It is separate from the climate-representation manuscript. The public replay now audits aggregates for the [environmental-organization screen](environmental_organization.md); it does not reproduce native geographical analyses, model fits or historical routes. No new *Spea* population division is established here.

The cost surfaces transform fitted environmental recording patterns into an assumed movement cost. They are not calibrated dispersal models. Local negative comparisons narrow particular hypotheses; they cannot rule out isolation elsewhere or in the past. Historical geometry, climate suitability, movement and ancestry require distinct evidence.

The provincial comparison used nine fixed fits in the three folds meeting prespecified common-climate support requirements. Two other folds lacked that support and were not fitted. Its small negative mean increment is conditional predictive evidence, not a test establishing niche equivalence or a definitive model of geographical variation.

Seasonal climate detail improves prediction under the tested comparisons, but this does not by itself identify ecological regions. The provincial-response, analogue and environmental-organization studies constrain particular explanations while leaving broader differences open. They reuse data and development geography; their outcomes are not independent confirmations of one overarching null hypothesis, and their differently defined scores should not be pooled.

The analogue summary above comes from the working archive and is not replayed by this public release. It retained 709 recorded climate cells and three equally sized geographically matched availability draws. Seventy pair screens passed, but 61 already matched under the literal-calendar threshold; they are overlapping exploratory contrasts, not 70 independent biological comparisons. Three control draws do not define a calibrated inferential null distribution.

## Priorities that would change the interpretation

Future work should begin with an independently motivated ecological or geographic contrast and observations capable of distinguishing its explanations. Depending on the question, those observations could concern breeding timing, water availability, habitat, physiology or population relationships. The completed screens supply no stable new group definition that warrants another clustering or predictor search.

Independent, replicated population sampling remains important for the proposed ancestry and population-history contrasts, including southeastern Oregon/northwestern Great Basin contact versus continuity and western/eastern basin histories. Existing reference coverage is geographically uneven and cannot test all proposed divisions. Genetic evidence is not a prerequisite for every ecological comparison.

Breeding-site distribution and hydroperiod are also plausible missing ecological dimensions. Their value should be tested with observations matched to that question; poor climate-model transfer alone does not identify ponds, predators or physiology as the cause. Contemporary pond information would not automatically transfer to the Last Glacial Maximum.

A stronger predictive result would use a genuinely fresh, locked evaluation after methodological choices are fixed. Repeating predictor, background or radius searches on the same inspected geographical folds cannot supply independent confirmation. Better population or habitat observations may be more informative than another sweep of those models.

## Scope of the contribution

The public climate study contributes a bounded empirical comparison: explicit simple controls, geographical allocation scores, retained adverse outcomes, and sensitivity to fitting and weighting. The exploratory biogeography work contributes candidate tests, a completed conditional environmental screen and constraints on interpretation. Holding sampled blocks fixed addresses some recording geography but also removes much broad rainfall-timing variation; the negative screen cannot establish ecological homogeneity. These are intermediate results in a living research project, without a demonstrated new lineage, adaptation or dated dispersal history.

The biological context for the northern/southern hammondii operational units is [Neal, Johnson and Shaffer (2018)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10592-018-1066-7). Independent physiological context includes [Gerick et al. (2014)](https://doi.org/10.1111/jbi.12261) and [Greenberg and Palen (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspb.2020.2273); their measured life stages and outcomes differ from the recording-density target used here.
