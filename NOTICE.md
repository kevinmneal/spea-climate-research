The original third-party publications and provider data are referenced rather than relicensed or bundled here.

The spatial thinning implementation follows a maximum-conflict-degree removal heuristic discussed in the spThin context; the R package was not executed. The soft-weight method translates the inspected enmSdmX distance-weight formula into Python; the R package was not executed. These method attributions do not imply endorsement by their authors. The manuscript's references identify both implementations and their sources.

CHELSA supplies the underlying climate product in the working analysis. CEC and Natural Earth supply the geographical definitions used there. Occurrence sources include the USGS western spadefoot release and additional frozen providers. Their raw datasets are not included. The [source/access guide](SOURCES_AND_ACCESS.md) and [machine-readable inventory](metadata/public_sources.json) distinguish source-specific licenses, unknown redistribution terms and exact-replay requirements. Bibliographic citations and the saved source specifications retain attribution; the repository license does not replace the terms of those original assets.

AI assistance was used for analysis code, computational review and writing. The repository records reproducibility checks at their actual level, and does not present automated review as independent peer review.
